kos

Ansible playbooks for a Lenovo ThinkPad T450s running CachyOS: systemd-boot, Intel Broadwell graphics, TLP and a small development toolbox.

RepositoryDecisions (ADR)Wikihanskruse.eu

Hardware

MachineLenovo ThinkPad T450s
CPUIntel Core i7-5600U (Broadwell, 2 cores / 4 threads)
GraphicsIntel HD Graphics 5500, no discrete GPU
Memory, disk12 GB, 512 GB SATA SSD (LUKS, btrfs)
SystemCachyOS, KDE Plasma, systemd-boot

What it sets up

  • Base: keyrings, upgrade, common packages, user groups, Neovim as editor.
  • Kernel: linux-cachyos, Intel microcode, boot options through sdboot-manage.
  • Graphics: Mesa with iris, Vulkan, and the i965 VA-API driver that Broadwell needs.
  • Laptop: TLP with battery charge thresholds, thermald, weekly TRIM.
  • Desktop: Flatpak, Chrome, Vivaldi, Proton Mail, Moonlight, Docker.
  • Development: PlatformIO, esptool, JBang, the JDK and jdtls, plus separate udev rules for ESP32 boards.

Every setup playbook has a teardown. Secrets are scanned with gitleaks in a pre-commit hook.

Usage

./controller-setup.yml          # ansible SSH key
./ansible-user-setup.yml -K     # localhost sshd + ansible user
./setup.yml                     # everything

Design choices, such as why mitigations are off and why TLP replaces power-profiles-daemon, are recorded in the ADRs.
